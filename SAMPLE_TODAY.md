# Set up your `/today` page

You build your `/today` page **one tab at a time** by telling your assistant, in plain
English, what you want to see and how often. Your assistant figures out which of your
agents can produce each tab, installs anything missing, schedules the refresh, and keeps
each tab current — you never name a sync, a file path, a slug, or any render code.

Send each of these as a message in your assistant DM. Add detail or your own preferences
in plain prose; the assistant will propose the setup and ask before it runs anything.

## Sample asks — nine tabs

- **On your calendar** — "Add a tab to my today page showing today's events plus where my time has been going lately. Keep my calendar current and refresh it every morning."
- **Needs your action** — "Add a tab that pulls together things needing my attention from my email and calendar, grouped by how urgent they are. Refresh daily with my latest data."
- **Wine deals** — "Add a tab showing the best current wine deals from what I track. Refresh daily."
- **Wine auction watches** — "Add a tab that checks Zachys and Acker auctions for wines on my watchlist and shows any that are in my price range. Refresh daily."
- **News** — "Add a daily news tab with the top AI, Taiwan, US, and local stories — a few each, with a short summary and a source link."
- **NYC** — "Add a daily NYC tab with picks for dining, nightlife, music, culture, deals, fashion, and outdoors — a few each, with a short summary and a link."
- **Receipts** — "Add a tab summarizing my business receipts from email and photos, grouped by purpose, with spend totals. Refresh daily."
- **Finance (MTD)** — "Add a tab showing my month-to-date spending vs. the same point last month, my top spending categories, and recent big transactions. Refresh daily."
- **Derivation layer** — "Add a small status tab showing each of my data pipelines — when it last ran and whether it has fresh rows. Refresh daily."
