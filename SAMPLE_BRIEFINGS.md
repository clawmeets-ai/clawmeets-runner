# Publish briefings to My Desk

Your **My Desk** is your personal dashboard. You fill it **one briefing at a time**
by telling your assistant, in plain English, what you want to see and how often.
Your assistant figures out which of your agents can produce each briefing, installs
anything missing, schedules the refresh, and keeps each briefing current — you never
name a sync, a file path, a slug, or any render code.

Send each of these as a message in your assistant DM. Add detail or your own preferences
in plain prose; the assistant will propose the setup and ask before it runs anything.

## Sample asks — nine briefings

- **On your calendar** — "Publish a briefing to My Desk showing today's events plus where my time has been going lately. Keep my calendar current and refresh it every morning."
- **Needs your action** — "Publish a briefing to My Desk that pulls together things needing my attention from my email and calendar, grouped by how urgent they are. Refresh daily with my latest data."
- **Wine deals** — "Publish a briefing to My Desk showing the best current wine deals from what I track. Refresh daily."
- **Wine auction watches** — "Publish a briefing to My Desk that checks Zachys and Acker auctions for wines on my watchlist and shows any that are in my price range. Refresh daily."
- **News** — "Publish a daily news briefing to My Desk with the top AI, Taiwan, US, and local stories — a few each, with a short summary and a source link."
- **NYC** — "Publish a daily NYC briefing to My Desk with picks for dining, nightlife, music, culture, deals, fashion, and outdoors — a few each, with a short summary and a link."
- **Receipts** — "Publish a briefing to My Desk summarizing my business receipts from email and photos, grouped by purpose, with spend totals. Refresh daily."
- **Finance (MTD)** — "Publish a briefing to My Desk showing my month-to-date spending vs. the same point last month, my top spending categories, and recent big transactions. Refresh daily."
- **Derivation layer** — "Publish a small status briefing to My Desk showing each of my data pipelines — when it last ran and whether it has fresh rows. Refresh daily."
